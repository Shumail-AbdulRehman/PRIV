# Product

## Users

Cleaning-company owners, location managers, and field staff in Pakistan. English/Urdu preference, reading fluency and digital confidence vary; optional spoken instructions should help adults complete real work respectfully. Administrators configure the company workspace and subscription plan, managers coordinate locations, shifts, attendance, and cleaning tasks, and staff complete attendance and task workflows from a mobile device.

## Product Purpose

Hygene Ops gives cleaning businesses one operational system for location management, workforce attendance, task scheduling, assignment, and photo-based verification. Success means managers can quickly understand today's operation, staff can complete required work with minimal friction, and subscription limits are predictable and clearly explained.

## Brand Personality

Professional, operational, and straightforward. The product should feel dependable and calm during repetitive daily work while communicating exceptions and limits without ambiguity.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative billing pages, generic marketing-style pricing cards inside authenticated workflows, excessive animation, vague feature names, and upgrade prompts that hide the exact limit a user reached.

## Design Principles

1. Keep operational work primary and subscription information secondary until a limit matters.
2. State allowances, current usage, and unavailable capabilities in concrete language.
3. Use familiar dashboard patterns and the existing Hygene Ops component vocabulary.
4. Explain blocked actions with a next step instead of presenting a generic error.
5. Keep plan enforcement consistent between the interface and the backend.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic status messaging, sufficient contrast, and reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on color alone to communicate plan access or limit states.

Core staff flow: check in → choose task → scan the assigned area QR → start cleaning → follow guided required photos → submit. Submission remains separate from final verification. Staff upload photos directly and retry failed uploads explicitly. Temporary pictures are not restored after leaving the screen. Keep upload receipt, checks and manager review distinct.
