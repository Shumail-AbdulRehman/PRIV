# v1 — reference-informed replacement

Direction: familiar SaaS onboarding, with a slim progress rail and three aligned plan panels; a clear selected-plan continuation replaces the rejected comparison ledger and receipt sidebar. The payment screen uses a separate order summary and provider handoff, with actual card collection delegated to Paddle.

Fixed Inter/Arabic fonts, blue #2556d8, slate #f8fafc and existing mark preserve the deployed project's identity. Public Linear and Notion layouts informed hierarchy, density and actions; their prices, logos, claims and auth features are not imported. See ../brief.md and the sourced research document.

The layout is an intentional adaptation to the user's stated direction. Brand and operational-task constraints override arbitrary font/hero/GSAP randomization. No marketing hero, trust logos or irrelevant image assets are added. A small abstract workspace illustration is labelled decorative and makes no numeric feature claim.

Local demo only: all prices, usage and dates illustrative, no provider/account APIs or persistent storage. Native radios are keyboard-operable, language switching keeps typed fields, and known/uncertain recovery are distinct. v1 has no animated reading values or modal entrance.
