# Direction contract

Task: create owner → choose capacity → review → Paddle test card → server-confirmed workspace.

Composition: setup rail + centered plan panels. The user-requested SaaS direction and fixed brand determine composition, typography and motion. AIDA/hero/GSAP/imagery are not applied to this operational payment task.

First viewport at 1440×900: slim slate setup rail; white main surface; 32px centered heading; monthly/yearly control; three equal plan panels with price, audience and three capacity lines; single continuation row directly after panels. No order sidebar stealing space from the choices.

Signature interaction: selecting a native radio changes the panel outline, its check, and the bottom selected-price/continuation text immediately. Mobile preserves that mechanism with compact horizontal panels. The interaction is immediate and readable rather than animated pricing.

Account uses one focused form. Payment changes to a two-column summary/provider handoff with test instructions before its primary action. Confirmation does not assert access from a browser event. Existing billing returns to familiar settings.

Checks: columns exactly fill three equal tracks with no empty cells; heading widths support at most three lines on phones; primary white-on-blue contrast passes AA; no false popularity/savings/trial claims.
